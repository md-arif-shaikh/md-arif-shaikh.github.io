{%- comment -%} Short date range, e.g. "Oct 20–21", "Mar 20 – Apr 20", "Aug – Dec", "Dec 28, 2024 – Jan 3, 2025". The year is left out when both ends share it (the page shows it in the year column). A range from the 1st to a month's end is shown as whole months (semester courses).
