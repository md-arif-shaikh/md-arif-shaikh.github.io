--- layout: page title: "Collaborators" permalink: /collaborators/ --- {%- comment -%} _data/collaborators.yaml is also read by the research page (by key), so country abbreviations are tidied here for display rather than in the data. Joint papers are counted from the short-author list (_data/short_author.yaml), whose author strings read "Surname, F. M.". A collaborator is matched as ", ." (e.g. "Das, T." for Tapas Kumar Das, which also catches "Ramos-Buades, A." and "van de Meent, M."). If someone appears differently in the bibliography, set `bib-name: "Surname, F."` on them in collaborators.yaml. Cards are ranked by joint papers (most first), then by name. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign people = site.data.collaborators | where_exp: "c", "c.name" | sort: "name" -%} {%- assign short_papers = site.data.short_author | where_exp: "p", "p.author" -%} {%- assign institutes = people | map: "institute" | uniq -%} {%- assign countries = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign joints = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign ranking = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for c in people -%} {%- assign country = c.country -%} {%- if country == "US" -%}{%- assign country = "USA" -%}{%- elsif country == "NL" -%}{%- assign country = "Netherlands" -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign countries = countries | push: country -%} {%- assign words = c.name | split: " " -%} {%- if words.size > 1 -%} {%- assign first_initial = words.first | slice: 0 -%} {%- assign bib_key = words.last | append: ", " | append: first_initial | append: "." -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign bib_key = c.name -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if c.bib-name -%}{%- assign bib_key = c.bib-name -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign after_space = " " | append: bib_key -%} {%- assign after_hyphen = "-" | append: bib_key -%} {%- assign joint = 0 -%} {%- for p in short_papers -%} {%- assign authors = p.author | replace: " and ", ", " | prepend: " " -%} {%- if authors contains after_space or authors contains after_hyphen -%} {%- assign joint = joint | plus: 1 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign joints = joints | push: joint -%} {%- comment -%} sort key "RR|PPP": joint papers descending, then alphabetical position {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rank = 99 | minus: joint | prepend: "00" | slice: -2, 2 -%} {%- assign position = forloop.index0 | prepend: "000" | slice: -3, 3 -%} {%- assign sort_key = rank | append: "|" | append: position -%} {%- assign ranking = ranking | push: sort_key -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign ranking = ranking | sort -%} {%- assign unique_countries = countries | uniq -%}
