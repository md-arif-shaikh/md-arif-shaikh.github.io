This is a late post about my last year's visit to [Seoul National University](https://en.snu.ac.kr/) and
[Ewha Womans University](https://www.ewha.ac.kr/ewhaen/index.do) in
October. I was invited by Prof Hyung Mok Lee to continue our
collaborative work on gravitational waves from eccentric mergers. Prof
Lee was generous to support my visit. During this trip, I also visited
Prof Chuglee Kim at Ewha Womans University and started a new
collaboration which we are about to finish.

My schedule was pretty tight, so this time I could only make a trip
to Suwon with Nicolas. As always, it was great to be back to Korea!
