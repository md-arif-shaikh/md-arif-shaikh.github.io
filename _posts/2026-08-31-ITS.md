ANRF for an International Travel grant to attend a workshop in Corfu,
Greece where I was invited as a panelist to discuss defining
eccentricity for gravitational wave astronomy. This is a topic that I
have been working on for the last few years and developed a dedicated
Python package
[gw_eccentricity](https://github.com/vijayvarma392/gw_eccentricity).

Unfortunately there was not enough time to apply for a visa and plan
my travel in time, so I was not able to use this fund.
